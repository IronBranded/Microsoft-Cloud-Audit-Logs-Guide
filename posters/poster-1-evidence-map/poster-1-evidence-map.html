No evidence does not automatically mean no activity occurred.

Before drawing a conclusion, establish:

  1. Was the evidence generated?
  2. Did collection or auditing require configuration?
  3. Was it configured at the time of the incident?
  4. Where was the evidence routed?
  5. What retention applied?
  6. Does the evidence still exist?
  7. Did the investigator have sufficient permissions to retrieve it?

Terminology

  • Azure Activity Log is control-plane evidence. It doesn't record every operation inside a resource.
  • Azure resources generate Resource Logs, but Azure Monitor does not collect them by default. A Diagnostic Setting or another documented collection configuration must exist for historical collection.
  • Diagnostic settings are a collection and routing configuration, not a log.
  • AzureDiagnostics is not the universal table; most services write to resource-specific tables.
  • Microsoft Sentinel correlates evidence routed to it; it doesn't produce the underlying evidence.
  • Defender evidence is security telemetry, alerts, and incidents, not an audit log.
